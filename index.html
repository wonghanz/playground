Under the hoodWhat is actually running

  1. Wave simulation on the CPU. A two-buffer height field solves the discrete wave equation (neighbours × ½) − previous at 240×N with 0.9715 damping and two substeps per frame, so ripples propagate and interfere instead of being a canned texture.
  2. Refraction from the surface gradient. The field uploads as a LUMINANCE texture; the fragment shader samples four taps to get a normal, then bends the light field by the local slope — that is where the glass thickness and the chromatic split come from.
  3. Specular tied to the sun. The light vector interpolates with the day phase, so the highlight direction, the caustic colour and the horizon band all move together rather than being a colour filter.
  4. Sound built from oscillators and noise. A looping filtered-noise swell with slow LFOs, and one-shots through PannerNode in HRTF mode, positioned by mapping screen coordinates into audio space.
  5. The game clock is wall time, not requestAnimationFrame. rAF is throttled to about 1 fps in a background tab, which would freeze a round mid-play; the rules run off a 60 ms interval and the render loop is only decoration.
